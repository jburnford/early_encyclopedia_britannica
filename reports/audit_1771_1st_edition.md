The 1771 First Edition is notable for being the original Encyclopaedia Britannica. Some characteristics to consider:

- Treatise-style organization means some letters have few standalone articles
- Cross-references are extensively used
- Original OCR from 18th century typography may have higher error rates
- Three-volume structure limits article counts per letter
