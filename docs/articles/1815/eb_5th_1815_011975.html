IMPERIAL

Volume 11 · 164 words · 1815 Edition
← IMPERFECT · Index · IMPERSONAL VERB →

something belonging to an emperor, or empire. See Emperor and Empire.—Thus we say, his imperial majesty, the imperial crown, imperial arms, &c.

**IMPERIAL Crown**. See Heraldry.

**IMPERIAL Chamber**, is a sovereign court, established for the affairs of the immediate states of the empire. See Chamber, and Germany.

**IMPERIAL Cities**, in Germany, are those which own no other head but the emperor.

These are a kind of little commonwealths; the chief magistrate whereof does homage to the emperor; but in other respects, and in the administration of justice, is sovereign.

Imperial cities have a right of coining money, and of keeping forces and fortified places. Their deputies assist at the imperial diets, where they are divided into two branches, that of the Rhine and that of Swabia. There were formerly 22 in the former and 37 in the latter; but there are now only 48 in all.

**IMPERIAL Diet**, is an assembly or convention of all the states of the empire. See Diet and Germany.
