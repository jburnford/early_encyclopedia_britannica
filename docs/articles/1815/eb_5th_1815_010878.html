HALLE

Volume 10 · 122 words · 1815 Edition

a little dismantled town of the Austrian Netherlands, in Hainault. The church of Notre Dame contains an image of the Virgin Mary, held in great veneration. E. Long. 3. 15. N. Lat. 50. 44.

handsome and considerable town of Germany, in the circle of Upper Saxony, and in the duchy of Magdeburg, with a famous university and salt-works. It belongs to the king of Prussia; and is seated in a pleasant plain on the river Sale. The inhabitants are stated at 14,000. E. Long. 12. 8. N. Lat. 51. 31.

free and imperial town of Germany, in Swabia, famous for its salt-pits. It is seated on the river Kocher, among rocks and mountains, in E. Long. 10. 50. N. Lat. 49. 6.
