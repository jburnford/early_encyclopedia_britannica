In this system the author has divided shells into the eight following classes, viz. 1. Tubulus. 2. Cochlidium. 3. Polytalamium. 4. Lepas. 5. Concha. 6. Conchoidea. 7. Balanus. 8. Echinus.

1. Tubulus, est testa tubulosa monotalamia, vel in lineam rectam extensa, vel incurva, vel contorta, vel aliquando ad spiram, fed irregulariter, accedens. Huic pertinent dentalia, entalia, folenes univalvi, &c.

2. Cochlidium, est testa tubulosa, monotalamia, conica, infrapris consitantem regularem, convoluta aliquando opercula praedita, sepius vero eo definita. Ad hanc classem spectant nautili tenuis five vacui vulgo dicti; aures marini, meritiæ, cochleæ, buccinae, musices, cassis, cylindri, volute, porcellaneæ, et omnes testæ turbinatæ, exceptis nautilo et anomia, ad classem tertiam referendis.

3. Polytalamium, est testa tubulosa polythalamia, conica, recta, vel in spiram regularem convoluta, cum syphunculo thalamos transeunte: huic reducendi nautili, anomia, litui, et orthocerata.

4. Lepas, est testa vacuoloa simplex, referens vaculum magis minutum cavum, orificio multum patente, ut patellæ simileaque.

5. Concha, est testa vacuoloa composita, bivalvis, id est quæ ex duabus componitur valvis, five vaculis magis minutis concavus in cardine articulatione quadam inter se junctis ut aperiri et claudi queant; ut chamae, mytili, tellinae, pinæ, ostrææ, pectines anomiae.

6. Conchoidea, est testa vacuoloa composita bivalvis, fed quæ praeteræ et aliquot minoribus portiunculis testaceis componitur, ut pholades anatiferæ.

7. Balanus, est testa vacuoloa composita, quæ pretium unicam testam majoræ alias portiones minores habet ex quibus componitur, ut balanus vulgo dictus.

8. Echinus, est testa vacuoloa composita unidice clausa; magis minutæ concava, duobus tantum foraminibus seu aperturis pro ore et ano perforata, externæ aculeis vel claviculis mobilibus testaceis armata.

IV. The system of Tournefort appeared for the first time, and was published from the author's manuscript fort in the treatise on conchology by Gualtieri. In this system shells are divided into three classes, viz. Monotoma, Ditoma, and Polytoma.

SYSTEM OF TOURNEFORT.

Testacea dicuntur quorundam animalium integumenta, quæ testæ seu lateris duritiam habent, et in quibus tantum, in testa animalia vivant.

Testacea autem omnia quæ hucufque in musæis curiosorum adversari et congeri solent, ad tres classes facile recovari possunt. Hæc enim vel monotoma sunt, vel ditoma, vel polytoma.

Monotoma testacea appellantur ea quorum testa indivisa est; ditoma quæ geminis constant tellis ad cardinem Classis Prima. Quae testacea monotoma complectitur. Testacea monotoma quorum testa indi- visa est, in tres families abeat: alia enim univalvia fuit, alia spiralia, alia fistulosa.

Familia I. Testaceorum univalvium. Monotoma u- nivalvia dicimus quorum testa simplex est in os amplius effusa. Gen. 1. Lepas. Gen. 2. Eruca.

Familia II. Testaceorum spiralia. Monotoma spi- ralia dicimus testacea quorum pars inferior in spiram contortuisset: horum autem spirae seu helices exterius patent, et simpliciter spiralia dicuntur, vel corum spirae intus recondituri, et convoluta dicuntur.

Divis. 1. Testacea monotoma simpliciter spiralia, seu quorum spirae exterior est. Gen. 1. Murex. Gen. 2. Murex alatus. Gen. 3. Murex aporrhais. Gen. 4. Murex venereus. Gen. 5. Murex pyramidalis. Ce- ratites. Gen. 16. Cochlea marina. Gen. 17. Nerita. Gen. 18. Auris marina.

Divis. 2. Testacea monotoma spiralia convoluta, quae cochleam interiorem habent vix foris conspicuum. Gen. 1. Concha venerea. Gen. 2. Concha persica. Gen. 3. Nautilus. Gen. 4. Conchilium.

Familia III. Testaceorum fistuliformium. Testacea monotoma fistulosa seu tubulosa, ut ex nomine patet, fis- tulae in modum tentantur. Gen. 1. Dentale. Gen. 2. Entale. Gen. 3. Tubuli marini.

Classis Secunda, quae testacea ditoma continet. Testacea ditoma semper ex duabus testis ad cardinem articulatis compinguntur, et vel arce undique claudun- tur, vel utrinque hiant; unde in duas families dividi possunt.

Familia I. Testaceorum ditomorum quae arce clau- duntur. Gen. 1. Concha. Gen. 2. Conchula. Gen. 3. Ostreum. Gen. 4. Mytilus. Gen. 5. Pinna. Gen. 6. Perna. Gen. 7. Pholas. Gen. 8. Peleten. Gen. 9. Peclunculus.

Familia II. Testaceorum ditomorum quae semper hiant. Gen. 1. Chamæ. Gen. 2. Solen.

Classis Tertia, quae testacea polytoma continet. Po- lytoma testacea dicuntur quorum testae ex pluribus parti- bus vel articulatis, vel per cartilaginem connexis com- pinguntur; unde in duas families abeat.

V. M. D'Argenville in 1742, published at Paris a treatise on Conchology with 33 plates. A second edition of the same work appeared at Paris in 1757. In this edition the number of the plates was increased to 41. A more splendid edition was published after the death of the author, by M. de Favanne de Montcerville father and son. This edition is extended to 3 volumes, two of which consist of letter press, and the 3rd contains the engravings, which are 80 in number, and are exe- cuted with great accuracy and elegance. But the de- scriptions of the genera and species only reach the 19th plate; so that the work which was published in 1780 is still unfinished.
