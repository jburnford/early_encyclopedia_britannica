ZYMOSIMETER

Volume 18 · 1,452 words · 1797 Edition
← ZYGOPHYLLUM · Index · ABATIS →

(formed from zymotic fermentation, and meter measure), an instrument propounded by Swammerdam in his book De Respiratione, wherewith to measure the degree of fermentation occasioned by the mixture of different matters, and the degree of heat which those matters acquire in fermenting, as also the heat or temperament of the blood of animals.
