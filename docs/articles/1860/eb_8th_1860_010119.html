LICENSE

Volume 13 · 1,143 words · 1860 Edition

or Licence, is a legal document granting permission to deal in certain specified articles of trade. A license is granted annually on application and on payment of the duty, except in cases where an abuse of privilege has been proven. Public-house licenses were granted in England as early as Edward VI. (1551); since which period this mode of imposing duty on excisable articles has been carried into several departments of trade. The annual revenue from licenses now exceeds £1,000,000.
