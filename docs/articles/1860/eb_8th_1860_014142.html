a market-town of Hungary, capital of a county of the same, on the Theiss, at its confluence with the Zagyra, in the midst of marshes, 29 miles S. by W. of Heves, and 49 S.E. of Pesth. It has the remains of an old cathedral; a church; an old Turkish mosque, now a chapel; a gymnasium; court of law; and extensive salt-works. There is an active trade in salt, timber, fruit, and fish. Pop. 10,617.
